# Hooks and retention

The first seconds decide whether the rest is watched.

- **Something happens in the first 2 s** (the pacing gate's `opening` check). A title
  card that is only text for 2 s is allowed to count (the card's text is an event); a
  black or static start is not.
- **Lead with a strong moment, not the intro.** For a montage over ~2 minutes, put the
  second-best moment first as a 3-5 s cold open, then the title card, then the build.
  (`kill_montage` does not do this itself - it is a spec edit: duplicate the clip's
  window at the top with a different `id`, shorten it, drop its slow-mo.)
- **A title says the promise.** `TECK - WARDOGS` over `12 KILLS / 2 NADES`: who, what,
  how many. No "insane", no "you won't believe".
- **Pace down the middle, not flat.** Alternate dense stretches (a run, a multi-kill)
  with a single long shot. A reel at one tempo for four minutes feels longer than it is.
- **The ending is the best thing, then stop.** A 2.4 s outro card is the maximum; a
  long outro is where viewers leave.
- **Retention is a hypothesis.** Do not claim a cut "will perform"; say what you did
  and why, and let the numbers from the platform correct the rules.
